# Requirements (functional & non-functional)
## Functional (example)
- Capture images of leaves/stems/fruits
- On-device classification into disease classes & healthy
- Local alerts (LCD, LEDs, buzzer)
- Log detections with timestamp + GPS
- Stream detection data to dashboard
- Manual & autonomous movement
## Non-functional (example)
- Runs offline (no network required)
- Inference latency <= 2 seconds (target)
- Operates for >= 2 hours on battery
- Safe power design with fuses and BMS
